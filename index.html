What the KPI is

The KPI is a short questionnaire that helps the Kelley Admissions Committee get to know you beyond your transcript. Kelley no longer offers automatic direct admission based on GPA or test scores. Instead, every student who submits the KPI gets a holistic review that considers high school performance, rigor of coursework, academic achievements, and potential for success at Kelley.

What Kelley says it learns from your answers

  • Leadership style

    How you tend to lead, organize, or contribute in a group.

  • Business interests

    What draws you to business and which parts of it.

  • Personal aspirations

    Where you want your education and career to take you.

  • Problem-solving

    How you approach a challenge or a decision.

Remember: the KPI complements the rest of your application. Your grades and the rigor of your courses are still central to Kelley’s review.

Key dates

You must apply to IU Bloomington first (Common App or Apply IU) and choose a Kelley business major. Then the KPI unlocks in your IU Admissions Portal.

  1. Nov 1
    IU Early Action deadlineApply to IU and select a Kelley business major.
  2. Nov 15
    Kelley priority KPI deadlineSubmit your KPI by this date to get the earliest decisions.
  3. Jan 15
    Early decisions releasedIf you met both early deadlines, you’ll hear from IU, and if admitted, get a Kelley decision: admitted, waitlisted, or pre-business.
  4. Feb 1
    IU Regular Decision deadlineLast call for your IU application.
  5. Feb 15
    Kelley regular KPI deadlineFinal date to submit the KPI and still be reviewed for direct admission.
  6. Apr 17
    Final Kelley decisionsRegular-round and waitlist decisions arrive by this date.

Earlier is better. Kelley makes offers on a rolling basis and the direct admit class has limited space, so submitting your KPI by the priority deadline may improve your chances. These dates are as Kelley currently posts them; always confirm in your IU portal.

Reflect before you answer

The KPI asks you to pick the response that feels most like you. That’s easier when you’ve already thought about how you actually operate. Spend 10–15 minutes on these prompts using real examples from your life. Nobody at Kelley sees this; it’s just for you.

Leadership style

Did you set the direction, organize people, step in when things stalled, or support the group from behind the scenes?

Business interests

Marketing, finance, starting something, how companies make decisions… Where did that interest start?

Personal aspirations

Don’t worry about a job title. Think about the impact, the people, or the environment you picture.

Problem-solving

Did you gather information, ask for help, try something quickly, or make a plan? What did you learn about yourself?

When you take the KPI

  • Choose the answer that describes what you actually do, not what you think sounds impressive.
  • Think of your real examples from above when two choices seem close.
  • Read each question fully before answering.

What to avoid

  • Trying to guess the “right” answer. Kelley says there isn’t one.
  • Having a parent or friend answer for you. It should sound like you.
  • Rushing it between classes. Give it a quiet, focused block.

Other paths into Kelley

Direct admission isn’t the only way. If you’re admitted to IU but not offered direct admission, you still have options.

Start as pre-business

Begin at IU as a pre-business student and apply to Kelley later through standard admission after meeting its academic requirements.

Pursue another IU major

Study in your second-choice major and add a Kelley minor or certificate.

6

Quick answers

Who is eligible for direct admission?

First-year students starting in a fall semester who choose a Kelley business major and submit the KPI. Summer and spring starts, transfer students, and students who don’t choose a Kelley major aren’t eligible.

Do recommendation letters count?

No. Letters of recommendation or support are not used in the direct admission review.

Can Kelley be my second-choice major?

To be considered for direct admission, you need to be admitted to IU with a Kelley business major. Check with IU Admissions on how first- and second-choice majors are used.

What if I submit the KPI after November 15?

You’ll still be reviewed if you submit by February 15, with a decision by April 17. Space is limited, so earlier is better.

Who do I contact with questions?

Kelley’s undergraduate admissions team at bschool@iu.edu, or your HelloCollege advisor.
